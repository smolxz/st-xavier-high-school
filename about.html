About Us

Who We Are

St. Xavier High School, Tihidi is a private educational institution dedicated to nurturing young minds from Nursery to Class X. We believe in creating a foundation for lifelong learning.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide quality education that focuses on academic excellence, moral values, and discipline, ensuring every student reaches their full potential.

👁️ Our Vision

To create responsible, confident, and compassionate individuals who contribute positively to society through holistic development.

🌟 Core Values

  • Integrity & Honesty
  • Respect for All
  • Commitment to Excellence
  • Social Responsibility
